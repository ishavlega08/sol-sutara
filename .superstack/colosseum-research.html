●Compliance / regulatory plays — Hard to demo, judges can't feel the urgency
●IoT integration — Hardware requirements add demo complexity that kills hackathon execution
●Long sales cycle B2B — Judges want to see users, not pilots that take 12 months to close

Implication for Sol Sutara: When entering Colosseum Eternal or future hackathons, lead with the recall simulation demo (instantly demoable, visually compelling, non-enterprise-dependent) rather than the compliance/enterprise pitch. Show the graph firing in real time — that's what wins.
