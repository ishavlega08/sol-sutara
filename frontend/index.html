The supply chain

The problem

Every recall is an archaeology project. Every audit is a spreadsheet rodeo. Every claim of "ethically sourced" is somebody's word, attached to a PDF, attached to an email — until it isn't.

We've shipped supply chains for forty years on the assumption that paper trails would scale. They didn't.

i.

Trust is a phone call.

You get a notice from a tier-1 supplier. They got it from tier-2. Tier-2's emailing tier-3. By the time the recall lands in customer hands, you've burned a week on the phone.

— "Three days of incident response is a Tuesday." — head of QA, EV OEM
ii.

Provenance is a PDF.

"Conflict-free cobalt." "Organic." "Single-origin." The certificate is real. Whether it tracks the actual lot in your warehouse — that's a different question, and nobody can answer it in seconds.

— Buyers will pay 17% more for verifiable origin (McKinsey, '24).
iii.

Audits are theatre.

Once a year, an auditor flies in, samples 4% of records, signs off, leaves. The other 96% is goodwill. Regulators are catching up — DPP in EU, FSMA 204 in US — and goodwill won't pass.

— EU Digital Product Passport: mandatory for batteries Feb 2027.

How it works

Sol Sutara turns each component into a compressed NFT on Solana. Each parent–child link is a co-signed edge. Trace runs against an indexed graph — not a chain walk — so a lookup that takes hours in your ERP takes tens of milliseconds here.

01

Register a part.

Mint a compressed NFT for any raw, component, subassembly, or finished good. POST /components from your ERP, or use the dashboard. Costs a fraction of a cent on Solana.

02

Link parts together.

When supplier and buyer both confirm a parent–child relationship, the edge is co-signed and committed. Two signatures, one immutable record.

03

Trace upstream — or down.

Pick any node. We walk the graph in both directions, returning every parent and child, with org signatures intact. Median 41 ms in our sandbox.

04

Recall, surgically.

Tag a contaminated lot. Every downstream child resolves automatically. Notifications go on-chain to every affected org. No phone trees.

The product

An ERP-shaped surface on top of Solana — components, links, traces, recalls, audit-grade exports. The chain is the substrate, not the UI.

01 · Mint

Compressed NFTs. Per part.

Every component is a compressed NFT. Cheap to mint, cheap to query, signed by your org's wallet. Metadata lives on Shadow Drive or IPFS — you choose.

// register a part await sutara.components.create({ id: "CM-18", name: "Cathode B-18, NMC-881", type: "Component", })
02 · Trace

One query. Whole graph.

Indexed parent–child relationships. Walk upstream, downstream, or both — trace.upstream(id, { depth: 7 }). Median 41 ms in sandbox.

03 · Recall

Blast radius, before you announce.

Tag a contaminated lot. We resolve every downstream child and quote you the cost before a single notification ships.

FAQ

If you've been around supply-chain blockchain projects before, you have justified scepticism. We share most of it. Here are the ones that come up every call.

Is this another supply-chain blockchain that doesn't ship?

+
Probably the right thing to ask. Difference: we're not selling a token. The product is a hosted graph + SDK. You pay in dollars (or USDC if you must). Solana is an implementation detail — you can use Sol Sutara without ever touching a wallet.

Why not just use a Postgres + audit log?

+
You can. But you can't make your suppliers sign rows in your database. The cryptography is what makes "tier-3 said this" actually verifiable instead of trusting your tier-1 to forward the truth. A central database belongs to one party; this graph doesn't.

Does my whole supply chain need to be on it?

+
No. Start with one tier-1 supplier and one product line. The graph grows where signatures grow. The parts you can't get signed are still recorded — just marked as unverified. Recall + audit work either way.

What about IP-sensitive supply data?

+
Component metadata can live off-chain (Shadow Drive, IPFS, your own S3). Only the hash and signature are public. The graph topology is opt-in — you can keep edges private to participating orgs only.

How are you priced?

+
$0 on devnet, free during build season. Production tiers start at $490/mo for 500k writes + 250k traces, or $0.0008 per write on top. Enterprise is custom — dedicated indexer + SAML. No tokens, no pre-sales.
